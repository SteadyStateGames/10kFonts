What do Core, Balanced and Experimental mean?
Core · 500 styles
Refined proportions, counters and spacing, with extra raster checks at small sizes. 184 supported characters, including selected Western Latin accents. Start here for more consistent mixed-case lettering.
Balanced · 250 styles
Alignment-adjusted versions of the original display designs, including corrections to A, V and W. 107 characters. These retain more of the original styling and do not have the full Core refinements.
Experimental · 9,250 styles
Display designs and effects with unusual shapes, outlines, cuts or shifted baselines. 107 characters. Best explored for titles and larger text.
